Our Story

🎉 Grand Opening · Surat

Tanatan Pan® is where heritage meets flavour. We craft every pan with the finest ingredients — from farm-fresh betel leaves to exotic gulkand — delivering a symphony of taste that lingers long after the last bite.

Located in the heart of Vesu, Surat, we've become the go-to destination for pan lovers seeking authentic taste paired with a luxurious contemporary experience. Each fold is a story. Each bite, a journey.
